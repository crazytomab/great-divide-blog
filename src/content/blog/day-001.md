Today was much harder than we thought it was going to be. We knew a lap of the Peak would be hard, but with fully rigged bikes for the Divide there were some long hike-a-bikes up very steep terrain.

Fantastic landscapes though, and definitely very good training.

By the end we were tired, but it felt like useful proof that the setup works.
